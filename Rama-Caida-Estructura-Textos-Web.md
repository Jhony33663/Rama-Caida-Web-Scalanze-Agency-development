### **Merchandising & Sales**

**Merchandising —** 

Retail merchandisers are the behind-the-scenes superheroes who turn shelves into captivating storybooks\! They master the art of product placement, ensuring every item shines in the spotlight to grab customers’ attention. From eye-catching displays to spot-on inventory management, their mission is to create an irresistible shopping experience that boosts sales and sparks brand love. **Neuro Sales —** 

At Rama Caida Product Sampling & Brand Awareness, we use neuromarketing strategies to create irresistible merchandising and sales experiences that captivate the brain and boost both brand awareness and sales. By tapping into unconscious triggers, we craft product displays and packaging that stimulate sensory appeal like vibrant colors, tactile textures, and engaging scents sparking instant emotional connections. We utilize eye-tracking and heatmap data to position products in “hot zones” where the brain naturally focuses, increasing the chance of purchase.

In sales, we leverage storytelling techniques that activate mirror neurons, making consumers feel connected and inspired by the brand’s message. Strategic use of social proof, scarcity cues, and gamification taps into the brain’s reward system, encouraging quicker decisions and repeat purchases. Our playful approach ensures the shopping experience isn’t just seen but felt, turning browsers into eager brand advocates and skyrocketing both recognition and revenue.

**SECCIÓN 3 — SALES STAFF**

## **Neuro Sales**

Neuro sales is a technique that applies neuroscience principles to understand how consumers make buying decisions. By studying brain activity, emotions, and cognitive processes, neuro sales helps sales professionals craft messages and experiences that better resonate with customers' subconscious motivations. This approach can boost sales by creating stronger emotional connections, enhancing trust, and influencing purchasing behavior more effectively. Using neuro sales strategies leads to tailored communication, improved customer engagement, and ultimately higher conversion rates.

With our own international certified staff we can create, manage and handle programs and marketing campaigns that work hand in hand with the merchandisers, sales staff, brand embassadors, product activators (Demo Reps) to not only boost your sales but to make your brand a day to day must have at home.

**Strategy Sales:**

* **Value:** We offer flexible packages tailored to your campaign size and duration, ensuring you get maximum ROI. Our tiered pricing includes options for short-term blitzes or long-term brand building.

* **Length of Service:** Choose from 1-month energizers (perfect for product launches), 3-month growth plans (for sustained momentum), or 6-month brand partnership programs (ideal for deep market penetration).

* **Why It’s Unique:** Unlike standard sales staffing, our bi-lingual reps are trained in cultural fluency and brand storytelling, making every interaction a mini brand ambassador moment. # **5\. Get to Know Us**

**SECCIÓN 1 — OUR ORIGINS**

## **Our Origins:**

Rama Caida was born out of the cumbersome experience I had many years ago while searching for someone to perform a demo program at supermarkets and outdoor events for a new beautiful line of grocery products where I used to work. It was of the outmost importance to yield the best return on investment without jeopardizing key aspects, such as: aesthetics and presentation of the product on display, neat and clean appearance of the staff performing the demonstration and good food safety practices.

None of the search results satisfied the quality standards, at the end it was decided to use different promotional tools for the lack of outstanding product demonstrators.

The frustration of those events later transformed into the opportunity to create an enterprise and to make a difference in the industry.

\-Francia Franco 2022
